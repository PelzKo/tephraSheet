## Aether

### Luminiferous aether
Aether is the particle that lets light travel. An aether resonator gathers it with radio frequencies and releases it
as free-floating plasma that can be shaped. Normally it is used for heat.

### Aether resonator and manipulator glove
- **Aether Resonator** (30 pr): heavy; it takes 2 hands to carry and is normally worn as a backpack. Light sources near
  it shine 5× as far (a candle lights 25 ft instead of 5 ft). Its carrier is not bothered by cold weather.
- **Manipulator Glove** (10 pr): a mostly metal glove to hold and shape aether harmlessly. While you wear it, your
  unarmed attacks use your aether. A prosthetic counts as a manipulator glove.
- Both count as basic adventuring gear and are free at character creation; augments on the resonator are paid as
  normal.

### Shaping aether
With a manipulator glove you can shape aether into a **melee or thrown weapon for 1 AP**. Released aether dissipates if
it isn't picked up within 1 AP; a dropped aether weapon must be picked up with your next AP; a thrown one dissipates
after it hits. Shaping without a glove deals **10 HP damage** to you, soaked only by your basic soak class; holding
the weapon afterwards is merely uncomfortably warm.

**Augmenting an aether weapon:** anyone who can augment weapons (normally Weapon Smith) may add augments for 1 AP per
augment slot: at most 3 augments, or 5 if you can make beta weapons (Beta Weapons). Allowed are Accurate,
Aerodynamic, Bone-Shattering, Damaging, Deflecting, Horrifying, Inspiring, Luminous (its effect ×5), Powerful,
Propelled, Reach, Returning, Signature Weapon (no Custom needed), Streamlined and Tangling. Aetherform Modules allows
every weapon augment you know.

**Passing it on:** the receiver needs an aether resonator too, or the weapon dissipates.

**Instant Draw:** with Instant Draw, shaping costs 0 AP; with Fast Shaping and Instant Draw, shaping and augmenting
cost 0 AP.

**Sundering:** an aetherform weapon can't be sundered unless the resonator feeding it is sundered.

### Syncing resonators
Syncing a resonator with an object (or undoing it) costs **3 AP**; it can be synced with several things at once (for
example a rifle and a glove). Someone who didn't build it (and wasn't taught) must roll a Sciences tier at least equal
to the highest marque on the resonator, one higher for a beta resonator.

**Aether as projectiles:** a resonator synced to a firearm, bow or crossbow makes it shoot aether instead of bullets,
arrows or bolts: normal weapon damage plus the aether effects. No custom ammunition with an aether-synced weapon.

### Light versus aether
Where there is no aether, no light source works (for example a Black Trap on someone holding an illumitorch; the
light returns with the aether). This also stops special light such as a farishtaa's halo.

### Aether versus aether
When two resonator users compete, both roll Sciences and tier it; the higher tier wins and the loser's ability fails.
On a tie both work, but only up to the midpoint between them.
