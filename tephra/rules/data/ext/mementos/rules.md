## Mementos

### Crafting with Luck
A memento can be almost anything you carry: a charm bracelet, a glass eye, an old bullet, an augmented saber or a pair
of goggles (rarely something large like an automaton or vehicle). An object with Sciences augments can also be a
memento; it is augmented fully and independently as a memento. Memento augments affect whoever holds the object, and
they work while it is merely carried.

- Each memento has **3 augment slots** (5 with Objects of Fortune). Multi-slot augments say so.
- **Mementos you create and maintain: 1 + 1 per 4 Luck** (Luck 1 → 1, 4 → 2, 8 → 3, 12 → 4, 16 → 5, 20 → 6,
  24 → 7). Create and augment them during downtime. Separated from you and the people connected to you for one
  downtime, a memento loses the connection and is "released into the world". Bound mementos given to others still count
  against the creator's maximum.
- **Buying:** market price per augment mQ I 25 · II 125 · III 625 · IV 3,125 princes; no-marque augments have their own
  price. You can't purposely buy a memento without being able to recognize mementos.
- **Stacking:** if you carry several objects with the same augment from a Spirit skill, you gain it only once, at the
  highest marque.
- **Two-marque augments** only have mQ I and mQ III; at 15 Luck you get mQ III.

### Binding (Memento Binding)
The memento must come into the target's possession in a meaningful way (a symbolic gift, tricking them into holding it
for long, attacking them with it …). You can bind one to yourself if it became symbolically important to you. It costs
no AP and happens as soon as the terms are met. The binding marque is your Luck marque; the target resists
subconsciously with Spirit (marques down). You can bind as many mementos as you can create. A bound object keeps
returning to its target:

| Binding marque | Return |
|---|---|
| I | Turns up during downtimes through strange deliveries or dropped into a bag or pocket by accident. If destroyed, it is fixed by someone nearby if possible; otherwise its parts arrive together, or it takes longer. |
| II | May be lost or destroyed in the morning but returns, fully repaired, before the day ends. Objects that are hard to repair may take a full downtime. |
| III | Returns within a few hours, often uncannily repaired; exceptionally rare or hard-to-repair objects before the end of the day. |
| IV | Even complex objects are not gone for long: back within minutes, maybe hours if complex (unless one of a kind). |

Bound objects crafted with Sciences rarely return repaired when they break: the broken parts return.
