## Explosives! – expanded rules

### Using and resisting explosives
Activating an explosive costs 1 AP; drawing and activating it together also costs 1 AP. Throwing it costs 2 AP. It
detonates at the start of your next turn and deals 10 damage per marque to everyone in the target space and the
8 spaces around it (soakable). Anyone caught can spend 1 AP reflexively for a Dexterity resist.

**Erratum:** each Dexterity tier above 1 ignores **one marque of the explosive's per-marque damage** (10 normally,
12 with Damaging mQ II, and so on), not a flat 5.

### Blind lobbing
Throwing at a target you can't see gives −4 Accuracy. On a miss, roll a d12: the item lands one space away in that
clock direction (3 = 3 o'clock).

### Disarming explosives
Disarming costs 3 AP and a Sciences roll. Making the required tier disarms it. Missing it by 1 tier does nothing;
missing it by 2 tiers sets it off at once.

| Sciences tier | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | fails; mQ III and IV explosives detonate |
| 2 | disarms mQ I–II; mQ IV detonates |
| 3 | disarms mQ I–III |
| 4 | disarms mQ I–IV |

### Object hardness
Tier 1 paper and cloth; tier 2 wood, ceramics and basic building supplies; tier 3 metals and ores; tier 4 adamantine
and reinforced steel. A common explosive destroys tier 1 objects (more with Ruinous).

### Targeting stationary objects
Roll a tiered Accuracy roll: a small object needs tier 2, an especially small one tier 3, a buzzing insect tier 4.

### Throwing further
Light explosives always reach 25 ft. To throw further, roll Brute: the range is tier × 25 ft, at −1 Accuracy per
10 ft beyond 25 ft. On a miss the explosive still lands in the chosen space.

### Mines
Setting an explosive as a mine costs 1 AP. It won't detonate in the first turn after activation; after that it goes
off as soon as someone moves into its space. The activator rolls Cunning to hide it (Concealable, Disguised and
Miniscule add to that roll); a searcher must meet or beat that roll to spot it. Floating or flying creatures don't
trigger it.

### Damage-free explosives
An explosive can be built to deal no damage at all. It has no base cost; only its augments cost money. This also
works for firebombs and squibs.

### Lockpicking with squibs
On a lock with a keyhole, roll Cunning plus the squib's damage. On a failure the lock can never be tried again, and
it makes noise.

### Firebombs
Crafted with Gadgetry (Firebomber). Thrown like a light throwing weapon; it lands in the target space even on a miss.
Activation 1 AP (drawing and activating together 1 AP), throw 2 AP. The fire starts within a second, so holding it
means taking damage. It deals **3 damage per marque** (3/6/9/12) to the target space and the 8 spaces around it,
burns at once and keeps burning until the end of the activator's next turn. It damages anyone who enters the area or
ends their turn there. Soakable; a 1 AP reflexive Dexterity resist on activation lowers the damage by one marque per
tier above 1 (and moves you to the edge or out). Anyone entering later can't resist, only soak. Augments that would
happen only once happen when the firebomb is first activated and reaches its target.

DIY → firebombs maintained: 1:6, 2:7, 3:7, 4:7, 5:8, 6:8, 7:9, 8:9, 9:10, 10:10, 11:11, 12:12. Market price
firebomb mQ I–IV 3/15/75/375 pr; firebomb augments 6/30/150/750 pr; crafting costs 1/5 (mQ I augment 1 pr 2 d).
3 augment slots (wood 2, organic 1); beta +2.

### Squibs
Crafted with Alchemy (Squib Producer). Thrown like a light throwing weapon; it detonates within seconds. Activation
1 AP (drawing and activating together 1 AP), throw 2 AP. It deals **5 damage per marque** (5/10/15/20) to the target
only, with no area. Normal Evade; soakable. Other squib effects are resisted with Dexterity unless stated. Every
augment on a squib uses your **Alchemy** marque.

DIY → squibs maintained: 1:10, 2:12, 3:13, 4:14, 5:15, 6:16, 7:17, 8:18, 9:19, 10:20, 11:21, 12:23. Market price
squib mQ I–IV 1/5/25/125 pr; squib augments 1/5/25/125 pr; crafting costs 1/5 (mQ I augment 2 d). 3 augment slots;
beta +2.

### Two-marque augments
Some augments only have mQ I and mQ III. You start with mQ I and get mQ III automatically at 15 points in the crafting
skill.

### The BombRats
Evanglessian sky pirates, once the Militarist "3rd Aerial Demolitions Unit" of Crimson Marshal Theo "Sicksmile"
Eddington, now led by Lisandra deGhast (flagship *Rat Queen*, an H-Class bomber, plus F-Class "Fury" bombers). They
are indiscriminate and have no honor, and they drop "Walking Bombs" (prisoners) onto ships. Never good allies.

- **Walking Bomb Serum:** an injected victim makes a tier 2 Spirit resist or attacks anything nearby without skill or
  training; they may resist again when attacking a friend; medicinal antitoxins help the roll.
- **Indifference Serum:** Walking Bombs ignore anyone injected with it (they see them as furniture).
- **Walking Bomb** (PAR 1 per 3): human, AP 2, HP 6, Wnd 6, Spd 30; cudgel 6/12/18/24. On death it explodes for 20
  damage in its space and all adjacent spaces (1 AP Dexterity resist, tier 3 to escape). Removing the bomb needs a
  tier 3 Sciences result; a failed attempt sets it off.
- **BombRat Pirate** (PAR 1): human level 3, AP 3, HP 39, Wnd 12; pistol, combat bombs, anti-vehicle bombs and
  ad-hoc grenades (Pyrotechnician, Beta Explosives, Blast Dodger, Instant Draw, Ad-Hoc Grenades).
- **Lisandra deGhast** (PAR 4): human level 7, AP 4, HP 72; immune to her own explosions; fights with the
  Blastwhammy and punch-packer squibs (Beta Squibs, Blast Impervious, Cluster Bombs, Quick Flick, Squib Producer).
