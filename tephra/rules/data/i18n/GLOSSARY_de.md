# German glossary for Tephra rule texts

Every translator (human or AI) must use these terms so all files read as one book.
Style: natural German, "du"-form when addressing the player, keep numbers, dice (d12), formulas and
abbreviations in brackets intact. Keep line breaks: the same number of lines as the English text
(the last 4 lines of a specialty effect are often the tier results T1–T4 and are parsed by position).

## Proper names
Place names, nations, religions, brand names (Spendo, Burgenhind Industries …), and invented words
stay unchanged. Specialty, augment, trait and story *names* are translated (short, punchy, like a
German rulebook would do it), e.g. Battle Theme → Schlachthymne, Victory Theme → Siegeshymne,
Marionette Strings → Marionettenfäden.
