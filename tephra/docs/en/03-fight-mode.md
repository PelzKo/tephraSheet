# Fight mode

Click **Start fight** in the bar above the sheet. The counter shows how many **action points (AP)** you have left this round.

- Spend AP with **−1 / −2 / −3** – on your turn *and* reflexively during other turns. They are subtracted right away. **+1** undoes a mis-click.
- **Switching things costs AP and is subtracted automatically:** entering a stance costs 1 AP (leaving is free; the stance you are already in when the fight starts is free). Maintained effects such as Battle Theme, Epic Dance, Blindside, Focused Support, the swarms (Parasite, Avian Wrath, Protect the Monarch, Colony of One), Belt Feeder, Flying Fortress and Hostile Maneuvers cost 2 AP to start. Bloodlust and Unquenchable Thirst cost 1 AP each (only while in the Berserker stance). The gnome's Growth Intensity costs 1 AP in each direction. If you don't have enough AP left, nothing happens and an info bar tells you why.
- Click **My turn** when your turn begins. Maintained effects take their **upkeep** (1 AP each, Concentrated Stream too). If you don't have the AP for all of them, a window lets you tick which ones to keep – the ticked costs can't add up to more than your AP. Everything else ends. With only one effect and no AP, it simply ends.
- Click **End my turn** when you are done: your AP refresh to the full amount and the next round starts.
- **End fight** switches the counter off. Outside fight mode, switching shows the costs in an info bar but subtracts nothing.
