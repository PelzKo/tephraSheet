# Fight mode

Click **Start fight** in the bar above the sheet. The counter shows how many **action points (AP)** you have left this round.

- Spend AP with **−1 / −2 / −3** – on your turn *and* reflexively during other turns. They are subtracted right away. **+1** undoes a mis-click.
- Click **My turn** when your turn begins. Ongoing performances (Battle Theme, Epic Dance) take their 1 AP upkeep automatically.
- Click **End my turn** when you are done: your AP refresh to the full amount and the next round starts.
- Starting Battle Theme during a fight takes the 2 AP right away.
- **End fight** switches the counter off.
