# The sheet

- **Tabs:** *Sheet* (page 1), *Specialties & Gear* (page 2), *Inventory & Effects*, *Story*. On a wide screen both sheet pages are shown side by side.
- **Breakdowns:** every value with a source (Accuracy, Evade, armor penalties …) opens a breakdown on hover or tap. Penalties are always shown with a minus.
- **Health:** *Damage* takes Hit Points first, then Wounds. **Wounds count up**: 0 means unhurt. Every time you take wounds damage, roll a called-shot location for the *wounded effect*. When all wounds are taken, every further hit is a *fatal effect*. Wounds heal 1 per day; a breather restores all HP.
- **Stance:** only one stance can be active; choosing another one ends the first.
- **Other:** ongoing performances such as **Battle Theme** and **Epic Dance** are not stances. Switching one on shows its cost (2 AP to begin, 1 AP every further turn). Linked specialties switch with it: Victory Theme adds the Battle Theme bonus to Strike, Never Stop the Dance adds the dance bonus to Defense. While active, the bonus is listed in the notes.
- **Situational:** toggles for conditional bonuses (e.g. "at 0 HP").
- **Money:** 1 king = 10 princes = 100 dukes. Click a money gear to gain, spend or move money.
- **Admin mode** lets you change values the rules normally fix (if the narrator allows it).
