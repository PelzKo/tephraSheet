# The sheet

- **Tabs:** *Sheet* (page 1), *Specialties & Gear* (page 2), *Inventory & Effects*, *Story*. On a wide screen both sheet pages are shown side by side.
- **Breakdowns:** every value with a source (Accuracy, Evade, armor penalties …) opens a breakdown on hover or tap. Penalties are always shown with a minus.
- **Health:** *Damage* takes Hit Points first, then Wounds. **Wounds count up**: 0 means unhurt. Every time you take wounds damage, roll a called-shot location for the *wounded effect*. When all wounds are taken, every further hit is a *fatal effect*. Wounds heal 1 per day. A **breather** restores all HP, ends the effects that last until a breather, ends fight mode and refills pet venom; one message lists all of it. The wound message has a button that opens the called-shot form.
- **Stance:** only one stance can be active; choosing another one ends the first.
- **Other:** ongoing performances such as **Battle Theme** and **Epic Dance** are not stances. Switching one on shows its cost (2 AP to begin, 1 AP every further turn). Linked specialties switch with it: Victory Theme adds the Battle Theme bonus to Strike, Never Stop the Dance adds the dance bonus to Defense. While active, the bonus is listed in the notes.
- **Situational:** toggles for conditional bonuses (e.g. "at 0 HP").
- The full text of the active stance and toggles is shown under the play bar; the ⓘ next to the others opens their details on a tap.
- **Money:** 1 king = 10 princes = 100 dukes. Click a money gear to *gain*, *spend* or move money *hand → bank* / *bank → hand*. You are asked before a balance goes below zero. *Give money* hands money on hand to another character you have unlocked.
- **Admin mode** lets you change values the rules normally fix (if the narrator allows it). Specialties and augments added there are marked **GM** and are never removed by rule checks. In admin mode the creation wizard can be reopened to edit choices: it only saves, it never rerolls or finishes again.
- **Saving:** if a tap could not be saved (no connection), a red bar says *Not saved – tap to retry*. Text you type in the journal, the notes and the *Found something?* row is kept on the device until it is saved.
- **Several devices:** the sheet reloads by itself when the character was changed on another device (not while you are typing or a window is open). If the journal or the notes were saved elsewhere in the meantime, your save is refused and both versions are shown.
- **Print** prints both pages plus an appendix with the full inventory, augments, notes, effects, specialty texts, companions and journal. **Pencil print** leaves HP, wounds, XP and money empty and shows stats without stance and toggle bonuses. **Print with level history** adds a page listing what was chosen at each level.
- **Export** downloads everything about the character (inventory, effects, companions, journal, level history) as a JSON file. The narrator can import such a file as a new character.
- **Offline:** a sheet you opened before can still be read without a connection (read only).
