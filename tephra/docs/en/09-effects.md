# Effects and called shots

The **Effects** list in the *Inventory & Effects* tab shows everything that currently affects the character. **Add effect** adds one:

- **Status effect:** type to filter the list (e.g. *Fatigued*) and pick one. Effects from the list apply their modifiers to the sheet. Text that matches nothing is added as a custom note without any effect on the values.
- **Called shot:** choose the location (1–12, as on the silhouette) and whether it is the normal, wounded or fatal effect. The rules text and modifiers of that location are applied. A normal hit to a hand drops the item held in it; some fatal effects lower the maximum wounds for good.
- **Remove** ends an effect. Each entry shows when it was added (in fight mode also the round) and whether it ends with a breather.
- When you take wounds damage, a message asks you to roll a location; its button opens this form already set to the wounded (or, once all wounds are taken, the fatal) effect. Rolling stays at the table.
- A **breather** ends the effects that last until a breather and lists them.
