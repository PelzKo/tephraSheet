# Companions

Animals and automatons have their own cards in the **Companions** tab (it appears once the character has one). **New animal** and **New automaton** are at the top of the inventory.

## Animals
Animals need the extension *Pets & Predators* (the narrator switches it on); otherwise there is no *New animal* button.

- **Start from a sample animal**, or build one step by step: size and niche, movement, special traits (1 SP each) and training stories, origin, wild, bonus SP and levels. **Save and recalculate** shows the result.
- The card shows the stats, the attack, SP and marque, the taming tier and the market price.
- **Damage** goes to HP first, then wounds; **Heal** restores HP; wounds have their own − / +.
- **Venoms:** *Use a venom dose* counts doses; *Refill* or a breather fills them up again.

## Automatons
*New automaton* is offered in the inventory once the character knows a specialty that crafts steamers, fuse boxes or clockwork; once the Companions tab is there, it has the button too.

- Choose type, marque, advanced core and whether it was built with DIY, its movement, the core process (slots), limbs with prosthetic augments (✓ = augments you know), fuse box specialties, and a weapon and armor from your inventory.
- **Damage** goes to HP first, then wounds; **Repair** removes wounds first, then HP. Buttons on the card switch the automaton's situational options.
- An automaton has no AP of its own: it acts on its controller's AP or its directives.
