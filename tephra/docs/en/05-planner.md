# Level-up and planner

## Level-up
With 12 XP, **Level up!** appears in the play bar (up to level 12).

- Choose the skill points: +2 in one skill and +1 in two other skills. Then the specialties you can learn appear; learn one. Its bonuses are added automatically.
- At levels 4, 8 and 12 you may **retrofit** one specialty for another of the same attribute (optional).
- **Before / after** compares your values with the result of your choice, including the new action points. **Random level-up** picks everything at random.
- The level-up uses up 12 XP. HP rise by the max-HP gain, wounds stay. When the augment stat allows a new augment, the **augments** page opens next; there you can also swap an augment for a new one of the same skill.
- A character with a higher starting level is built up one level at a time; this costs no XP.
- Each level-up is listed in the level history (Story tab) and the narrator keeps a copy of the character from before it.

## Planner

Open **Planner** from the sheet tabs to plan coming levels before you reach them.

- For each future level choose the skill points (+2 / +1 / +1), a specialty, augments to learn and a note.
- At levels 4, 8 and 12 you can also plan a **retrofit** (replace one specialty with another of the same attribute).
- The plan table checks whether each planned specialty, retrofit and augment can be learned at that point, including the augment capacity (✓ or the missing requirement).
- **Preview** compares your values now with the values after all planned levels.
- When you level up, the plan for that level is **preselected** – you can still change everything. The level-up page shows a before/after table of your choice and tells you when it differs from the plan; planned augments are marked on the augments page. Plans of levels you have passed are removed.
